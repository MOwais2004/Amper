FAQ

Can't find it here? Our team answers in under 4 minutes, 24/7.

Talk to a human
Is Amper a bank?

No. Amper is a financial technology company. Accounts are held with regulated partner banks and electronic money institutions, and your money is safeguarded or insured depending on region.

How long does it take to open an account?

Most businesses are verified in under 10 minutes. You'll need your company registration details and an ID for each owner with more than 25%.

Which countries can I pay?

You can send to 160+ countries in 40 currencies. Most popular routes arrive in seconds; the rest typically within one business day.

What exchange rate do you use?

The mid-market rate — the one you see on Google — plus a transparent fee from 0.25% to 0.6% depending on your plan. No markups hidden in the rate.

Can I connect my accounting software?

Yes. Amper syncs with Xero, QuickBooks, NetSuite and Sage, and exports clean CSVs for everything else.

Is there a minimum balance?

No minimums, no monthly fees on Starter, and you can cancel any time.
